# Booking Approval: You Choose Who Gets In

Not every event is first-come, first-served. Sometimes you need to check skill level, verify membership, or simply make sure the right people are in the room. That's why we've launched **Booking Approval** — a simple way for organisers to review and approve attendees before their spot is confirmed.

Players tap **Request to Book** instead of **Book Now**. You review each request in your Organiser Hub. And here's the part that matters most: **their payment method is not charged unless you accept them.**

<!--more-->

## Why Organisers Love It

Booking Approval puts you back in control without adding friction to your workflow:

- **Curate your crowd** — approve the right mix of players for competitive sessions, members-only events, or skill-based groups
- **No awkward refunds** — since cards aren't charged until you approve, you won't need to refund players you turn away
- **A fair window to decide** — you have **48 hours** to review each request before it's automatically declined
- **Works with forms** — review a player's form responses before you hit approve

## What Players Experience

From the player's side, the flow is straightforward and transparent:

1. They select tickets and click **Request to Book**
2. They see a clear message: _"Organiser approval required. Your card won't be charged until you're approved."_
3. After checkout, they get a confirmation that their request was submitted
4. You approve or reject — and they're emailed either way

If approved, their card is charged and they receive a booking confirmation. If rejected, the hold on their card is released and the spot goes back on sale.

## How to Turn It On

Booking Approval is available on paid events with Stripe connected. Enable it when creating an event, or toggle it anytime from your event's **Settings** tab.

Once enabled, head to **Organiser Hub → your event → Attendees** to review pending requests. You'll see **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Rejected** tabs — approve or reject with a single click.

## Try It Out

Running a session where you want to hand-pick participants? [Enable Booking Approval in your event settings](/docs/organisers/events/booking-approval) and take control of who joins.
